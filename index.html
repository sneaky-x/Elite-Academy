About ELITE

Nurturing the Next Generation of Hospitality Leaders

At ELITE Hotel Academy, we believe that great hospitality is an art — one that combines skill, passion, and professionalism. Founded with a vision to elevate Sri Lanka's hospitality industry, we deliver world-class training that meets international standards.

Located in the heart of Marawila, our academy provides a comprehensive learning environment where students gain hands-on experience in all facets of hotel operations. Our expert faculty brings decades of real-world hotel experience to every lesson.
